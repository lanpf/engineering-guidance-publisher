# 3.1.8（2026-09-28）

## 范围

基于 3.1.7（a47c0b2）约束 `Require.that` 判定谓词的输入纯度；只新增一条规则，不改变既有规则语义、Skill 名称、scope 或引用路由。

## 新增规则

- `JAVA-REQUIRE-THAT-PURITY-001`（required/topic）：A `Require.that` predicate takes the passed-in `value` as its only input: it may call `value`'s own methods or reference `static final` constants, and must not capture other variables, method parameters, `this` fields, or external mutable state. Cross-variable or state-dependent decisions are explicit guards handled as technical preconditions; never rewrite them as `Require.that` calls.
- 源来源：`standards/bp_validation.md#显式检查`，插入在显式检查工具选择顺序规则之后。
